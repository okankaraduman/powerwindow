Tomorrow's price and scheduled loads

When is tomorrow's electricity price published?

It depends on the daily publication. Once the curve is available, Power Window stores it in the backend and reuses it for later visits.

Why check tomorrow's electricity price?

For scheduled loads: EV charging, water heating, laundry, dishwashing, drying, heat pumps, or air conditioning.

Is tomorrow always cheapest overnight?

No. Overnight can be good, but Spain also has many competitive solar-hour periods. Look at the daily curve before deciding.

Source: PVPC data published by Red Electrica. Power Window is independent. Tomorrow's prices appear only when the series is available.
