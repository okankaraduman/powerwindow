Compare PVPC with electricity offers for a flexible household

The comparator is designed for homes with EV charging, cooling, laundry, or other loads that can move into cheaper hours.

What does PVPC with flexible usage mean?

It is a simulation of the regulated case: base usage keeps the peak, shoulder, and valley split, while flexible kWh are placed in the cheapest PVPC hours.

Can I compare electricity supplier offers?

Yes. Enter the energy price, power term, monthly fee, and taxes from a real offer, then mark whether those prices already include taxes.

Does the ranking depend on referral links?

No. The ranking should come from estimated monthly cost. Future referral or affiliate links will be labelled as commercial and will not decide the order.

Power Window uses PVPC data published by Red Electrica for the regulated case. Editable offers are assumptions stored in the browser and are not financial advice or a completed commercial offer. Privacy.
