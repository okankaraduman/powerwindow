Hourly electricity prices, EV charging, and flexible loads in Spain

Power Window uses hourly PVPC data published by Red Electrica to help shift flexible electricity use such as EV charging, laundry, dishwashing, or cooling.

What is the best time to use electricity in Spain?

There is no single best hour every day. The app compares the selected day's hourly PVPC series and finds the cheapest window for the selected duration.

Can I use it for EV charging?

Yes. The EV planner estimates energy from battery size, current charge, target charge, and charger power, then finds the lowest-price hours for that session.

Is the estimated cost a full electricity bill?

No. It is an estimate for deciding when to shift flexible usage. Contracted power, meter rental, taxes, retailer margin, and other bill items can vary by contract and fiscal territory.
