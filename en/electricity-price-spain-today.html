Using today's electricity price

What is the cheapest electricity hour in Spain today?

The minimum hour appears in the summary. For multi-hour usage, use the best continuous window rather than a single cheap hour.

Are the prices shown in €/kWh?

Yes. The hourly values are converted to €/kWh so they match how most households compare electricity prices.

Can I use this for EV charging?

Yes. This page gives a quick reference; the planner lets you set car model, battery, target charge, and charger power.
